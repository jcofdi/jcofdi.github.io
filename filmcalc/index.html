GEP Film Lab

Coating colour for GEP visors, glass and solids (opaque paint, leather, metal). Films use the same Belcour–Barla evaluation as gepEvalIridescence; visors and glass can also use a dichroic quarter-wave stack. Set the coating the way you would in the textures, then read off the values or share them.

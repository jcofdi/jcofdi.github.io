Thickness range 0–400 nm (GEP_FILM_THICKNESS_MAX 400), 1.6 nm per B step. Normal map: four-channel BC7 with ordinary (non-normalised) mips. Dielectric substrate F0 is 0.03, as on the cockpit path (0.035 with cockpit GI). The shader treats the substrate's phase as a dielectric's, so metal substrates may need a few B steps of adjustment in-sim.
